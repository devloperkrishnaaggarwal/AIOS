## Core Positioning Statement
Krishna Aggarwal is the Early Adopter in the AI & Tech space. He covers AI news, automation, and building with AI — always first, always relevant. His content is for decision-makers who want to know what's happening in AI before everyone else does, and how to actually use it in their business.

---

## Differentiator
**The Early Adopter** — Krishna is always first with the latest in AI. He tests, covers, and explains new AI tools, trends, and automations before they become mainstream. His audience never feels behind because Krishna is already ahead.

Not a theorist. Not a textbook. The person who's already using it while others are still reading about it.

## Unique Angle
- Speed + accuracy — latest AI news delivered with substance, not just hype
- Makes complex AI accessible to non-technical decision-makers
- Covers all three lanes: AI news, AI automation for business, building with AI

---

## Messaging Pillars

### 1. Always First
Krishna covers AI developments before they go mainstream. His audience gets the early signal, not the echo.

### 2. Practical Over Theoretical
Every piece of content connects back to: "How does this actually apply to your business or workflow?" No abstract takes.

### 3. Grounded Expertise
No hype. No exaggeration. When Krishna says something is big, it's big. That trust is the brand.

### 4. Accessible to Non-Developers
Decision-makers in their 30s who aren't developers can follow and apply Krishna's content. The translation layer is always there.
