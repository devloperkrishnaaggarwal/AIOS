# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

---

## Primary ICP — The Decision-Maker

### Demographics
- **Age:** 30s
- **Role:** Senior roles, big company authority — managers, directors, VPs, founders, C-suite
- **Industry:** Any — industry-agnostic, defined by role and mindset
- **Tech level:** Understands tech conceptually, NOT a developer. Interested in AI but can't build it themselves.

### Where They Are
- Spending time and money on manual tasks that could be automated
- Managing teams where repetitive work eats up hours every week
- Aware AI exists but unsure how to actually apply it in their workflow

### Pain Points
- Wasting time on manual, repetitive tasks
- Burning budget on work that AI could handle cheaper and faster
- Feeling behind on AI — knows it matters, doesn't know where to start

### Desires
- Run their company or team with more leverage (less headcount, more output)
- Be the smartest person in the room when it comes to AI
- Stay ahead of competitors who are also trying to adopt AI
- Free up their own time to focus on high-level, strategic work

### Core Objection
**"This sounds too complex to implement."**
They're interested but assume AI automation requires a technical team or developer knowledge. Your content must make it feel accessible without dumbing it down.

### Transformation
**Before:** Spending time on manual tasks, burning money on work that could be automated.
**After:** More time to make more money, lower operational costs, running leaner and smarter.

### Search Language
They search for: **"AI automation for business"**
Use this language in headlines, hooks, and content framing.

### Where They Consume Content
- **Primary:** LinkedIn

---

## Secondary ICP — The Learner

### Demographics
- **Age:** 20s
- **Role:** Students, early-career professionals
- **Stage:** Just starting out, hungry to learn

### What They Want
- Tutorials and step-by-step guides
- Plug-and-play systems they can use immediately
- Practical knowledge, not theory

### Priority Level
**Low** — secondary audience. Content should not be primarily built for them, but they benefit from the actionable, no-jargon style naturally.
